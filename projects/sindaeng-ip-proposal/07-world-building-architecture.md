# Pages 11–16 — World-Building Architecture

## Core World Statement

> SINDAENG lives in a world where emotions do not disappear. They take root, grow, and reshape the environment.

The world is designed as a visual extension of Emotion Bloom™. Each location is not merely a backdrop; it is an emotional ecosystem that changes according to SINDAENG’s inner state.

---

## Page 11 — The World of SINDAENG

### Headline

**A world grown from feelings.**

### Core Copy

SINDAENG’s world begins from a small emotional seed.
A quiet thought can become a flower.
A lonely night can become a moss forest.
A spark of courage can awaken a goblin companion.

### Visual Direction

- Hero character centered in a minimal landscape
- Six environmental zones radiating outward
- Warm neutral background with vivid emotional accents
- Keep the character readable before the world becomes complex

## Page 12 — Emotion Forest

The primary narrative space where emotional seeds become living forms.

### Visual Language

- Oversized flowers, moss, mushrooms, wind ribbons, tiny spirits
- Organic rounded shapes
- Slow movement, soft atmospheric light

### Story Use

- Short-form episodes
- Healing and discovery stories
- Bloom and Moss product collections

### Product Use

- Blind-box display diorama
- Plush and stationery patterns
- Exhibition environment

## Page 13 — Dream Village

### Function

The everyday social space of the IP.

### Key Locations

- SINDAENG’s Room
- The Tiny Market
- Bloom Workshop
- Quiet Tea House
- Roof Garden

### Narrative Role

This is where small, relatable daily events become emotional stories. The village should feel contemporary, whimsical, and lightly inspired by Korean spatial details without becoming a historical recreation.

### Cultural Integration Rule

Use Korean inspiration through:

- Color rhythm
- Roofline and window proportions
- Textile patterns
- Market objects
- Seasonal rituals

Avoid using traditional motifs as decorative stickers without narrative relevance.

## Page 16 — Weather Hill

### Function

A landscape where emotional weather becomes visible.

### Weather-to-Emotion Mapping

| Weather | Emotional meaning | Visual behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Light rain | Quiet sadness | Small cloud follows SINDAENG |
| Wind | Restlessness | Ribbons and hair objects move first |
| Fog | Uncertainty | Background loses detail |
| Snow | Stillness | Motion slows and sound softens |
| Sunbreak | Relief | Warm light appears before expression changes |

### Key Rule

Weather must reflect emotional state without becoming a simplistic one-to-one symbol. The environment may contradict the face to create emotional depth.

---

## World Expansion Logic

Every new world zone must satisfy three conditions:

1. It represents a clear emotional or narrative function.
2. It can support at least one content format and one product family.
3. It preserves SINDAENG’s core visual identity rather than overpowering the character.

## Design Guardrail

The world may grow dramatically, but SINDAENG remains the visual anchor. The environment should feel like the character’s emotion made visible—not a separate fantasy franchise pasted around the character.
