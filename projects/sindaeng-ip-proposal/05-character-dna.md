# PAGE 05 — CHARACTER DNA

## Character identity

**Name:** SINDAENG  
**Korean:** 신댕  
**Category:** Emotional Designer Toy Character  
**Origin:** Korea  
**Core device:** Emotion Bloom™

## One-line definition

> A quiet child whose emotions grow visibly above the head.

감정이 머리 위에서 자라나는 무심한 아이.

## Personality

SINDAENG is observant, independent and unexpectedly active. The face often appears tired or indifferent, but the character is not cynical. SINDAENG notices small things, acts before speaking and reveals feelings through objects, creatures and weather that grow above the head.

### Key traits

- Quiet, but not passive
- Deadpan, but emotionally rich
- Independent, but warm
- Curious, but rarely impressed
- Playful in unexpected moments
- Comfortable with contradictory feelings

## Character contrast

| Visible exterior | Inner reality |
|---|---|
| Neutral face | Active imagination |
| Small body | Expanding emotional world |
| Few words | Strong visual storytelling |
| Traditional references | Contemporary designer-toy attitude |
| Still pose | Sudden energetic movement |

## Fixed design DNA

These elements should remain recognizable across all products and media.

1. Oversized rounded head and compact body ratio
2. Heavy straight fringe framing the eyes
3. Half-lidded or restrained eye shape as the default expression
4. Small mouth positioned low on the face
5. Modernized Korean silhouette with broad sleeves or layered shapes
6. Saekdong-inspired color blocking used with controlled saturation
7. Chunky, toy-like footwear
8. A clear attachment zone above the head for Emotion Bloom™
9. Soft 3D material language with a collectible vinyl-toy finish
10. A readable silhouette even when reduced to icon size

## Variable design elements

- Emotion Bloom™ objects and creatures
- Seasonal palette
- Costume details
- Props and occupation themes
- Environment and world transformation
- Expression intensity
- Product material: vinyl, resin, plush, digital or animation

## Non-negotiable recognition rule

At least **five fixed DNA elements** must remain visible in every licensed adaptation. The face, fringe, body proportion and Emotion Bloom™ attachment logic should never all change at the same time.

## Default emotional state

**Quiet Curiosity**

SINDAENG’s baseline is not sadness. It is a neutral state of observing the world before reacting.

## Signature behavior

- Pauses before reacting
- Suddenly runs when genuinely interested
- Allows small creatures to sit on the head
- Collects ordinary objects as emotional memories
- Returns to a neutral face after exaggerated action

## Visual direction

- Use one large official front render.
- Add four cropped detail circles: eyes, fringe, sleeve/color blocks and footwear.
- Place a small silhouette comparison along the bottom.
- Label fixed elements with minimal technical callouts.
