# Pages 21–28 — Art-Toy Collection Structure

## Collection Strategy

SINDAENG art toys should be developed as a collectible system rather than as isolated costume variations.

Each figure combines four structured layers:

1. Core SINDAENG body
2. Emotion Bloom™ family
3. Bloom Code™
4. Edition-specific material and story

This architecture allows consistent recognition while supporting long-term collectability.

---

## Page 21 — Product Architecture

### Core Formats

| Format | Suggested scale | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Mini Blind Box | 7–10 cm | Entry and repeat purchase |
| Standard Vinyl | 15–20 cm | Main collectible |
| Premium Resin | 25–35 cm | Art collector and gallery |
| Mega Figure | 60 cm+ | Exhibition and flagship retail |
| Plush Hybrid | 15–30 cm | Lifestyle and emotional accessibility |

### Design Rule

Do not scale every detail proportionally. Small formats require simplified Bloom silhouettes, stronger color blocking, and fewer fragile elements.

## Pages 22–23 — Series 01: First Bloom

### Positioning

The first commercial collection introduces the Emotion System™ in its clearest form.

### Suggested Line-up

1. **EB-00 Quiet Seed** — neutral core figure
2. **EB-01 Yellow Bloom** — joy and gentle anticipation
3. **EB-02 Moss Night** — rest and quiet loneliness
4. **EB-03 Tiny Goblin** — mischief and courage
5. **EB-04 Blue Bird** — curiosity and hope
6. **EB-05 Rain Cloud** — sadness and waiting
7. **EB-06 Moon Orbit** — dream and imagination
8. **Secret: Overflow Garden** — Level 3 Bloom

### Chase Ratio Principle

The secret figure should offer a meaningful change in silhouette, material, or narrative—not merely a new color.

### Packaging Story

Each box includes:

- Bloom Code™ card
- Emotion description
- One-line micro-story
- QR link to a short animation

## Page 24 — Series 02: Goblin Mood

### Theme

Korean-inspired playful spirits reinterpret courage, irritation, curiosity, and mischief.

### Visual Direction

- Stronger red, orange, teal, and black accents
- Rounded horn motifs
- Toy-like rather than frightening monsters
- Dynamic asymmetric silhouettes

### Product Opportunities

- Blind box
- Glow eyes variant
- Soft-rubber accessory
- Seasonal Halloween collaboration

### Cultural Guardrail

The collection should draw from the playful logic of dokkaebi without directly reproducing a single historical artifact or stereotypical costume.

## Page 27 — Edition Ladder

### Standard

- Core colorway
- Widest distribution
- Clear emotional concept

### Seasonal

- Spring, summer, autumn, winter interpretations
- Limited production window

### Artist Edition

- Guest artist reinterprets the environment and Bloom material
- Core face and body rules remain protected

### Cultural Edition

- Korean seasons, markets, folklore, textiles, and local collaborations
- Narrative relevance required

### Museum / Gallery Edition

- Larger scale
- Numbered production
- Certificate and process book

### Collaboration Edition

- Partner brand can influence material, setting, or accessory
- Partner cannot replace SINDAENG’s identity system

## Page 28 — Collection Economics Framework

This proposal does not present fixed revenue forecasts until manufacturing quotes, channel margins, demand tests, and minimum order quantities are confirmed.

### Variables to validate

- Sculpting and tooling cost
- Minimum order quantity
- Paint complexity
- Packaging and insert cost
- Defect allowance
- Freight and customs
- Retail and distributor margin
- Secret-figure production ratio
- Marketing and content cost

### Recommended Test Path

1. Produce one 3D master model
2. Prototype three Bloom variants
3. Release digital polls and short-form tests
4. Manufacture a limited preorder edition
5. Use sell-through data to define blind-box quantity

### Investment Principle

Build evidence before building inventory.
