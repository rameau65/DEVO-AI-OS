# PAGE 08 — PROPORTION & SHAPE LANGUAGE

## Proportion system

The final numeric ratio must be measured from the approved master image or 3D model. Until measured, label all values as provisional.

### Provisional visual hierarchy

1. Head: primary recognition mass
2. Hair and fringe: facial frame
3. Upper garment: broad color and silhouette mass
4. Legs: short supporting structure
5. Shoes: weighted visual anchor
6. Emotion Bloom™: variable vertical extension

## Shape language

- Head and cheeks: rounded, soft and stable
- Fringe and eyes: horizontal, calm and restrained
- Sleeves and garment panels: broad geometric blocks
- Shoes and ornaments: chunky, playful forms
- Emotion Bloom™: asymmetric organic growth

## Rule

The base body should remain visually calm so the Emotion Bloom™ can carry movement and narrative complexity.

# PAGE 09 — FACIAL EXPRESSION SYSTEM

## Expression principle

SINDAENG’s emotions are visible in both the face and Emotion Bloom™. The face must change enough to read clearly, but it should retain the character’s restrained identity.

| Expression | Eyes | Brows | Mouth | Cheeks/body | Emotion Bloom™ response |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral | Half-lidded | Relaxed | Small flat mouth | Still | Small dormant seed |
| Joy | Curved or narrowed | Slight lift | Open smile or raised corners | Light bounce | Flowers open, petals rise |
| Sadness | Lowered gaze | Inner brows rise | Small downward curve | Shoulders drop | Mushroom or cloud bends downward |
| Anger | Sharper upper lids | Brows lower inward | Compressed mouth | Lean forward | Horns, heat or goblin energy expands |
| Surprise | Eyes open wider | Raised | Small open circle | Body pulls back | Objects pop upward |
| Shyness | Side glance | Soft | Closed small smile | Hands gather inward | Tiny buds appear near the sides |
| Excitement | Eyes compressed with energy | Lifted | Broad smile | Strong running or jumping pose | Full growth and particle burst |
| Fatigue | Heavy lids | Flat | Slightly open | Slumped | Bloom droops or sleeps |

## Critical correction

Do not reuse the same neutral face underneath different expression labels. Every expression needs visible changes in eye aperture, eyebrow direction and mouth silhouette.

# PAGE 10 — POSE & MOTION LANGUAGE

## Essential poses

- Neutral standing
- Walking
- Running
- Jumping
- Sitting comfortably
- Perching on an edge
- Looking upward
- Holding an Emotion Bloom™ creature
- Sudden delighted reaction
- Return to neutral

## Motion principle

SINDAENG’s comedy and appeal come from contrast:

> Long stillness → sudden energetic action → immediate return to a neutral face.

## Animation timing reference

- Observation pause: 12–24 frames
- Anticipation: 4–8 frames
- Burst action: 6–12 frames
- Settle: 8–16 frames
- Neutral reaction hold: 12 frames or longer

Frame counts are creative starting points, not mandatory technical standards.
