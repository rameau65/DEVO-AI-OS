# Page 01 — Cover / Brand Statement

## Primary Copy

**SINDAENG™**  
*Korean Emotional Designer Toy Brand*

**Emotion grows on the head.**

Every emotion blooms into a story.  
모든 감정은 하나의 이야기가 되어 자란다.

**Everyone has a SINDAENG inside.**

Korea · 2026 · IP Proposal Version 1.0

## Visual Direction

- Format: 16:9 landscape master, adaptable to A4 landscape.
- Background: warm white or very light warm gray.
- Hero image: official full-body SINDAENG, centered slightly right of the vertical axis.
- Large negative space around the character.
- Wordmark at upper left; tagline aligned below it.
- Version and origin information at lower right.
- Use one thin horizontal rule or a restrained color strip derived from the character palette.
- Avoid crowded grids, decorative frames, and excessive product imagery on the cover.

## Brand Tone

Quiet, premium, emotionally intelligent, playful beneath the surface, Korean without becoming folkloric or souvenir-like.

## Design Principle

The cover must communicate three ideas within three seconds:

1. This is a distinctive character IP.
2. Emotion is expressed through the head ecosystem.
3. The brand is suitable for art toys, animation, and global licensing.
