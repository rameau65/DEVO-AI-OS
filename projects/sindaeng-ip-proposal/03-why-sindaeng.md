# PAGE 03 — WHY SINDAENG?

## Page purpose

Explain the emotional and commercial reason SINDAENG should exist now. This page should move the proposal from visual attraction to brand necessity.

## Headline

# The world is full of smiling characters.
# People are not always smiling.

## Korean copy

세상에는 늘 웃고 있는 캐릭터가 많습니다.

하지만 사람은 언제나 웃고 있지 않습니다.

무심함, 지루함, 외로움, 호기심, 설렘, 장난기처럼 이름 붙이기 어려운 감정도 일상의 일부입니다.

SINDAENG은 그 감정을 억지로 긍정으로 바꾸지 않습니다. 대신 감정이 존재한다는 사실을 귀엽고 낯선 방식으로 보여줍니다.

## English copy

The character market is full of happiness.

Real life is full of mixed feelings.

SINDAENG gives quiet, awkward and contradictory emotions a visible form—without forcing them to become cheerful.

## Core statement

> SINDAENG is not another happy character.
> It is a character people can project themselves onto.

## Why now

### 1. Emotion-first consumption

Audiences increasingly choose characters that reflect identity, mood and personal taste rather than simple cuteness.

### 2. Short-form compatibility

The contrast between SINDAENG’s restrained face and the dramatic Emotion Bloom™ creates a complete visual joke or emotional beat in 5–15 seconds.

### 3. Collectible scalability

One base character can generate multiple art-toy collections through emotion, object, season and world transformations.

### 4. Korean visual identity

Modernized hanbok silhouettes, color-blocking inspired by saekdong, and goblin or spirit motifs provide a Korean foundation without turning the character into a traditional costume mascot.

### 5. Language-light global storytelling

Emotion Bloom™ communicates through shape, motion and color, allowing stories to travel without heavy dialogue or translation.

## Visual direction

- Left side: restrained portrait or full-body render with neutral expression.
- Right side: six small Emotion Bloom™ silhouettes emerging from the same character.
- Large empty space and short lines of copy.
- Avoid market charts on this page; it is an emotional thesis page.

## Presentation narration

“Most character brands sell a permanent smile. SINDAENG begins where that smile ends. Its value comes from visualizing mixed emotions in a way that is instantly readable, collectible and expandable.”
