# SINDAENG Emotion System™

## Core Idea

SINDAENG rarely changes her basic expression dramatically. Instead, emotions emerge above her head as living visual forms. These evolving forms are called **Emotion Blooms™**.

Emotion → Emotion Bloom™ → Story → Product → Content → Licensing

## Emotion Bloom Levels

### Level 1 — Seed
A tiny sign of emotion appears: a bud, spark, drop, horn, cloud, eye, or small creature.

### Level 2 — Growth
The sign develops into a recognizable object or companion and begins interacting with SINDAENG.

### Level 3 — Overflow
The bloom expands across the hairstyle, silhouette, costume details, and nearby props.

### Level 4 — Emotion World
The surrounding environment transforms into a themed world suitable for animation episodes, figure dioramas, exhibitions, and seasonal campaigns.

## Design Rules

- SINDAENG's base facial identity remains recognizable in every series.
- Emotion Blooms amplify emotion but must not obscure the face completely in standard products.
- Every bloom needs one dominant silhouette, one hero color, and one companion behavior.
- Korean visual references should be reinterpreted rather than copied literally.
- Each code must support at least one story, one short-form action, one art-toy treatment, and one low-cost good.
