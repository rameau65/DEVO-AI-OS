# Pages 17–20 — SINDAENG × Emotion Bloom™ Relationship Rules

## Purpose

Emotion Bloom™ is not a hat, costume, or random decorative object. It is a living visual expression of SINDAENG’s emotional state.

This section defines how the character and Emotion Bloom™ interact across illustration, animation, art toys, merchandise, and licensing.

---

## Page 17 — Core Relationship

### Principle

> The face stays restrained. Emotion Bloom™ reveals the emotional depth.

SINDAENG’s expression may change, but the character should not rely on exaggerated facial acting alone. Emotion Bloom™ carries additional information through shape, scale, movement, and environmental response.

### Four Communication Layers

1. **Face** — subtle immediate feeling
2. **Body** — physical reaction and intention
3. **Emotion Bloom™** — emotional intensity and symbolic meaning
4. **Environment** — wider emotional consequence

### Example

A sad scene should not be solved only by adding tears.

- Eyes lower slightly
- Shoulders become heavier
- Moss Bloom bends downward
- Nearby light becomes softer

## Page 18 — Growth Rules

### Level 0 — Dormant

- No visible Bloom or only the signature base form
- Used for neutral introductions and product identity

### Level 1 — Seed

- One small emotional symbol appears
- Suitable for subtle daily episodes and entry-level goods

### Level 2 — Bloom

- Multiple elements form a readable emotional composition
- Standard level for animation, blind boxes, and seasonal collections

### Level 3 — Overflow

- Bloom extends beyond the head silhouette
- Strong body and environmental response
- Reserved for major story beats and chase figures

### Level 4 — World State

- The surrounding environment transforms
- Used for key visuals, exhibitions, premium editions, and episode finales

### Scale Guardrail

The Bloom may exceed the character silhouette, but the face must remain visible in primary brand applications.

## Page 19 — Behavior Rules

### Movement

Emotion Bloom™ should react a fraction earlier than SINDAENG’s body when revealing an emerging feeling.

Examples:

- A flower bud opens before the smile
- Goblin horns rise before the character runs
- A small cloud forms before the shoulders drop
- A bird turns toward danger before SINDAENG notices

This creates a signature animation rhythm and gives the Bloom a semi-autonomous personality.

### Material Behavior by Series

| Series | Material feeling | Motion behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Bloom | Petal, fabric, soft vinyl | Opens, floats, sways |
| Moss | Velvet, flocking, soft foam | Settles, droops, slowly expands |
| Goblin | Gloss vinyl, rubber, lacquer | Bounces, snaps, rotates |
| Spirit | Translucent resin, light | Hovers, pulses, trails |
| Weather | Cloud, mist, clear plastic | Forms, dissolves, drifts |
| Cosmos | Iridescent resin, metallic finish | Orbits, flickers, rotates slowly |

## Page 20 — Licensing Rules

### Required

- Preserve SINDAENG’s core face and proportion
- Assign a defined emotional meaning to each Bloom design
- Use an approved Bloom Code™
- Maintain visual hierarchy: character first, Bloom second, environment third
- Document color, material, and movement behavior

### Prohibited

- Using random decorative objects with no emotional meaning
- Replacing the character’s entire head with an unrecognizable object
- Turning every emotion into a broad smile or extreme facial distortion
- Mixing multiple Bloom families without a narrative reason
- Using Korean motifs only as surface decoration without context

### Approval Checklist

Before approval, every licensed variation must answer:

1. What emotion does it represent?
2. Which Bloom family and code does it belong to?
3. How does the face change?
4. How does the body change?
5. How does the Bloom move or behave?
6. Can the character still be recognized in silhouette?

## Brand Test

Remove the logo. If the product no longer reads as SINDAENG, the design needs revision.
